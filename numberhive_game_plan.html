Number Hive · 9-Month Bridge

Game Plan (loose)

For James. Working draft — the full model, wireframes, flows & matrix live in the applet (numberhive_architecture_fixed.html) + the model summary.

01 The bet, in one line

The product is loved; the funnel is broken. Build a free, viral, play-first top-of-funnel that lets anyone — especially teachers — experience the magic in two minutes, and prove it can acquire at scale before we build the rest.

If we can't get a lot of users through the free B2C mouth, nothing else matters. So we test that first, cheaply.

02 Why this, why now

  • ~£2–3k MRR, subs-only. Once teachers use it, they love it. The bottleneck is getting them to see the value.
  • The three frictions this kills:
    • Sales calls blocked by admin gatekeepers → product-led, bottom-up: a teacher adopts by playing; the school deal follows.
    • Teachers won't evaluate a tool → they play a 2-minute game, not sit a demo.
    • No automated school process (promo-code hack) → School super-admin replaces it (fast-follow).
    • Confusing onboarding → "just play," no setup.
  • Assets we're not using enough: ~15k educator emails; a genuinely loved & differentiated product; TTRS as both positioning and proof ("Number Hive is the best application of number operations — we just do drill-and-kill") — which also proves schools/kids already love competitive maths-fact games, de-risking Hive Clash.

03 The model (resolved)

  • Two roles: Player & Admin. One gate: free to play, pay to see and manage (one boolean per hive). One world, umbrella-contained (minors under a teacher/parent umbrella). Tiers = price brackets (Single / Group / Class / School), not feature gates.
  • Gameplay + data generation are always free. Win-share is public for players, teacher-only for students. Hive IQ is personal (never in an admin's reports).

04 The 6-week MVP — "prove the mouth"

Goal: test the riskiest assumption — can the free B2C funnel acquire & spread? — as cheaply as possible. Not the full monetisation (the current product already serves paying teachers).

Ship

  • Free instant-play game (no account) — the magic, in 2 minutes.
  • The viral loops: share-your-win card (players, public), invite-a-friend (host / join / play & pass), the profile hub.
  • Light account + the player home.
  • Lean conversion: let early teacher converts use the existing product/gate — don't rebuild it yet.
  • Heavy instrumentation: activation, D1/D7 retention, K (invites + conversion), per-loop.
  • Launch motion: point the 15k list (sequenced touches, not one blast) + organic + in-product loops at it.
Explicitly NOT in the 6 weeks (fast-follow): ranked Hive Clash (heaviest build), School super-admin automation, rebuilt teacher reporting, cosmetics/seasons.

05 How we judge the bet

Anchor metric: activated players = played ≥1 full game and came back once. (Not raw "plays.")

Quality gates — these matter most

SignalMinimum to imply it works
Activation (arrivals → full game)≥ 50–60%
D7 retention≥ 20–25%
K (viral coefficient)≥ 0.5 and trending up

Tripwires

🟢On the 20k curve → caught fire → lean in (ranked, more loops, maybe a little spend).
🟡3–8k, K ≥ 0.5 and rising → engine works, under-seeded/under-tuned → keep going, tune the loop.
🔴< 2k and/or K ≈ 0 → mouth/magic isn't landing → fix onboarding/magic/seed before building more.
The maths to watch: K = i × c (invites per user × invite→signup rate). Amplification on any seed = 1 / (1 − K) — K=0.6 turns 1 user into 2.5, K=0.8 into 5. We don't need K>1; we need it as high as we can push it. Keep viral cycle time short — loops fire right after a match (minutes, not days). Judge the slope and loop quality, not the headline number.

06 The 9-month bridge — loose sequence

Weeks 0–6 · MVP
Ship the free game + loops + instrumentation; launch to the 15k.
Weeks 6–12 · Read & tune
Measure K/retention; tune the share/invite flow to push K; fast-follow ranked Hive Clash (single-player now seeds the pool); start the impact report from existing data.
Weeks 12–24 · Build the conversion (if 🟢/🟡)
Polish the gate; School super-admin to replace the promo-code; automate the school process; harden teacher reporting; weaponise TTRS positioning.
Weeks 24–36 · Scale what works
Push the winning loops; decide B2C's "own life" via the tripwire; institutional/expansion motion; maybe a small efficacy study.
Throughout
Fletch (5h) on owned content/positioning + impact report; Jono (20h) on ops/billing/school process.

07 Positioning & proof (low budget)

  • Line: "Number Hive — where kids apply number facts strategically. Not drill-and-kill." (TTRS's own words about us.)
  • Proof, cheaply: publish an aggregate impact report from our own pre/post Buzz + mindset data — owned marketing that directly attacks "teachers can't see the value." Frame as indicators, not an RCT (don't over-claim). Chase a small school/uni study later.
  • Channels: the 15k list (sequenced), teacher communities, and the in-product viral loops doing the heavy lifting.
