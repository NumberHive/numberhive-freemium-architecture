The gate
Gameplay is identical for students at every educator tier. Subscriptions gate what the educator can see and manage — never what the student can play.
Silent data
Free educators generate competency data from day one. The subscription unlocks the view (🔒), not the collection.
Two worlds
Public multiplayer and hive management never overlap. The only link between worlds is the teacher-transfer bridge.
