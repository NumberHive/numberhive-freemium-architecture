Why Each Layer Matters
Within Classes
Each student who joins makes the hive more competitive — driving engagement for everyone already there.
Between Teachers
A teacher seeing another class's results is pulled in by curiosity. Organic B2B referral with no sales cost.
Across Schools
Inter-school competition creates institutional adoption. The product spreads district-wide without a rep.
Player to Player
Hive IQ means students want to climb the ladder at home. Every player becomes a recruiter — B2C from B2B.
Hive Clash
An integrated competitive layer with asymmetric upside — built into the ecosystem from day one.

⚡ Hive IQ

A global ranking system — one ladder, every player, everywhere.
🌍Global, not local. Students compete against every Number Hive player — not just their class. The ranking means something because it has scale.
📈Self-motivating. Hive IQ creates intrinsic drive to improve and climb. Students play outside school hours without being asked.
🏷Shareable identity. "My Hive IQ is 1,340" is something students tell friends. Rankings become social currency — the viral hook.
🎓Teacher controlled. Teachers can enable or disable Hive Clash per hive — full institutional control, no friction for adoption.
🔓Open to all. Free B2C users access Hive Clash too — global Hive IQ ties school and non-school players together on one ladder.

Two Scenarios — Both Work
Scenario A
Engagement + Lead Engine
Hive Clash increases student engagement and retention, strengthens network effects, and drives word-of-mouth that feeds back into school acquisition. It supports and accelerates the core B2B model.
Scenario B
Meaningful Consumer Product
Hive IQ gains traction beyond the classroom. Hive Clash becomes a standalone consumer product — with its own audience, growth loop, and potentially its own revenue stream. The B2B model remains the foundation.
The Key Point
The strategy works in either case.
The only uncertainty is not whether Hive Clash belongs — it does. The unknown is how big it becomes.
