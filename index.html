Why Network Effects Change Everything
Within Classes
Each student who joins makes the class hive more competitive — increasing engagement for everyone already there.
Between Teachers
A teacher seeing another class's results is pulled in by curiosity and peer pressure — organic B2B referral.
Across Schools
Inter-school competition creates institutional adoption — the product spreads district-wide without a sales team.
Between Players
Student-led challenges via Play-a-Friend turn every player into a recruiter — B2C growth from B2B gameplay.
