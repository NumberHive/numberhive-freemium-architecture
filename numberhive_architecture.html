Integrated, not detachedHive ClashBridges B2B and B2C
Hive Clash bridges B2B and B2C. It may become a lead generator, a retention layer, or something much larger.
Role A · Lead generator / engagement layer
  • Keeps students returning
  • Creates challenge links
  • Grows the player base
  • Feeds teacher discovery
Role B · Standalone upside (if it takes off)
  • Ads · cosmetics · premium stats
  • Tournaments · sponsorships
  • Parent subscriptions
  • App-store / B2C potential
Integrated from day one. The uncertainty is not whether Hive Clash belongs, but how large it becomes. Treat B2C as upside — not the core business assumption.
The frameStrategic summary
Every user can play. Every player can share. Every share can surface a teacher. Every teacher can unlock insight.
Free play creates reach. Network effects create growth. Hive Vault creates conversion.
Teacher-first monetisation. Multi-channel discovery.
Not every user must convert — and that's the point.
Some convert — teachers & schools subscribe.
Some refer — they bring a teacher or family in.
Some just play — and that's fine.
Some bring in students — widening the base.
Some bring in teachers — surfacing buyers.
Some create B2C growth — Hive Clash spread.
All are valuable because they increase reach, engagement, data, school discovery, and network density. The main business remains school subscriptions — but discovery is no longer limited to schools.
